Doodlemaar privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Doodlemaar (previously called Inkwit) is a party game played between phones in the same room. It is made by Koinworx. This policy explains what the app does with information, which is very little.

What Doodlemaar Does Not Do

  • No account, sign-in, or registration.
  • No servers. The app never sends your data to us or to anyone else over the internet.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking identifiers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, or location history.

What Stays on your Phone

  • The name you type to be shown to other players.
  • Game settings (rounds, round time, word packs).
  • A random device identifier and per-room reconnect tokens, so you can rejoin a room if the connection drops. They identify your phone to the host phone only, never to us.
  • Crash reports, if the app crashes: the error message, the technical stack trace, the app version, the Android or iOS version, and short notes about what the app was doing (for example "hosting room ABCD"). They are stored only on your phone. You can read, share, or delete them under Settings → Diagnostics. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it yourself.

What Other Players in the Room See

While you play, the host phone relays to everyone in the room: your display name, your guesses, your emoji reactions, your score, and, when it is your turn, your drawing. This stays within the room's local connection (Wi-Fi, hotspot, or Bluetooth) and is discarded when the room closes. The word being drawn is sent only to the drawer.

Permissions and why

  • Camera: only to scan a room's QR code. No photos are taken or stored.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: to host or join a room over Bluetooth when there is no Wi-Fi.
  • Location (Android 12 and older): Android requires it to scan for Bluetooth devices and to create a hotspot. Doodlemaar does not read or store your location.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13 and newer): to create a hotspot for the room.
  • Notifications: to show "Doodlemaar is hosting" while your phone hosts a room.
  • Run in background / battery optimisation: so a room you host does not end when the screen locks.

Children

Doodlemaar has no accounts, chat with strangers, purchases, or advertising. Players only ever connect to phones in the same room. The "Kids" word pack contains age-appropriate words.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update and the date above changes.
